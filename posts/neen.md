i recently bought the domain `neenerweener.ca`. not entirely sure what i want to do with it yet, but i think its a nice escape from being scared to actually iterate on my "official" personal website.

software is an interesting field to work in. during my adolescence, i was drawn to programming because it was something fun, something that occupied a lot of time and actually forced me to think. however, at some point, my interest in software engineering became an interest in finding a job.

i can't put my finger on exactly when this changed for me, but it was definitely somewhere near the end of high school. i stopped building software for myself, and started viewing myself through an outsiders lens. everything i built had a purpose, and that purpose was deeply rooted in the fact that i wanted to be perceived as a "good" software engineer.

i would like for that to change. i've been having more and more fun with software development recently, building a bunch of personal projects, and i'd like to talk about them somewhere where my name, major, and my resume aren't plastered everywhere. i guess that's what neener is.
